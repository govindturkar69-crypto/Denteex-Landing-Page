# Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about running your practice on Denteex, organized by topic. If you don't find your answer here, reach out via [Contact Sales](/) or the in-app support widget.

## Getting Started

**How long does setup take?**
Most solo practices are fully onboarded within a day. Multi-doctor and enterprise rollouts typically take 1–2 weeks, including staff training and data migration, with a dedicated onboarding specialist on Growth and Enterprise plans.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. Denteex runs entirely in the browser — desktop, tablet, or mobile — with no local software to install or maintain.

**Can I try it before committing?**
Yes. Start a free trial directly from the pricing section; no credit card is required, and you can explore the interactive 3D odontogram and scheduling tools immediately.

## Data & Security

**Is patient data encrypted?**
Yes. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for full details on our security practices and HIPAA Business Associate obligations.

**Who owns our patient data?**
You do. Denteex processes data on your behalf as a service provider; you retain full ownership and can export your data at any time.

**Is Denteex HIPAA compliant?**
Denteex is built to support HIPAA-compliant workflows and signs Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with U.S. clinics. Compliance is a shared responsibility — your practice's internal policies and staff training also matter.

## Migrating from Another System

**Can you import our existing records?**
Yes. Our onboarding team handles bulk imports of patient records, treatment history, and scheduling data from most common practice management tools at no extra cost on Growth and Enterprise plans.

**Will we lose historical X-rays or documents during migration?**
No — imaging and document migration is part of the standard onboarding process and is verified against your source system before cutover.

## Billing & Plans

**Can we change plans later?**
Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from account settings. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

**Is there a contract lock-in?**
No. All plans are billed monthly or annually with no long-term lock-in — cancel anytime and export your data.

**Do you offer annual discounts?**
Yes, annual billing saves approximately 20% compared to monthly billing across all plans.

## Features

**What is the interactive 3D odontogram?**
A visual charting tool that lets dentists mark tooth-by-tooth conditions (cavities, fillings, crowns, and more) in real time during an exam, replacing paper charts and disconnected imaging tools.

**How do automated reminders work?**
Denteex sends configurable appointment and follow-up reminders via email, SMS, and WhatsApp, reducing no-shows without manual front-desk effort.

**Can we manage multiple locations from one account?**
Yes, on the Growth and Enterprise plans, with a consolidated multi-location analytics dashboard.

## Support

**What support is included?**
All plans include support during business hours; Enterprise plans include 24/7 priority support and a dedicated account manager.

**Where can I find setup guides?**
See our [Quick Start Guide](/docs) for a walkthrough of the odontogram, scheduling, reminders, and billing modules.
