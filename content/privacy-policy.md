# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 25, 2026**

Denteex ("Denteex," "we," "us," or "our") provides cloud-based dental practice management software. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when dental clinics ("Customers") and their patients use our platform (the "Service").

Denteex is built to handle dental practice data responsibly, including Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and personal data as defined by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This policy is written to be readable by clinic administrators, patients, and regulators alike.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers data processed through the Denteex web application, mobile-friendly patient portal, and associated APIs. It does not cover third-party sites linked from our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

**From clinics (Customers):**
- Account information: staff names, roles, email addresses, phone numbers.
- Billing information: invoicing details processed via our payment processor (we do not store full card numbers).
- Clinic configuration: locations, operating hours, service catalog.

**From patients, on behalf of clinics (as a data processor):**
- Identity and contact details: name, date of birth, address, phone, email.
- Clinical records: treatment history, odontogram charting data, digital X-ray images, prescriptions, insurance claims.
- Appointment data: booking history, reminder preferences, attendance records.

**Automatically collected:**
- Device and usage data: browser type, IP address, pages visited, session duration, feature usage — collected to improve reliability and detect abuse.

## 3. How We Use Information

We process data to: provide and maintain the Service; enable scheduling, charting, billing, and automated reminders (email, SMS, WhatsApp); provide customer support; detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell patient data. We do not use patient clinical data to train third-party AI models without explicit, separate consent from the Customer.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the Service to Customers), legitimate interests (security, product improvement), consent (marketing communications, where required), and legal obligation (tax and healthcare recordkeeping requirements).

## 5. Our Role Under HIPAA

For clinics operating in the United States, Denteex acts as a **Business Associate** as defined by HIPAA. We enter into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with each Customer that processes PHI through the Service. Our safeguards include encryption of PHI in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), role-based access controls, audit logging of PHI access, and workforce training on PHI handling.

## 6. Data Security

We apply defense-in-depth: encrypted storage and transport, least-privilege access controls, regular access reviews, rate-limited and validated API endpoints, and security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options) on all web traffic. No system is perfectly secure; see our incident response commitments below.

## 7. Data Retention

Clinical records are retained for the period required by applicable dental recordkeeping regulations in the Customer's jurisdiction (commonly 7–10 years, longer for pediatric patients), or until the Customer instructs deletion, whichever is longer. Account and usage logs are retained for up to 24 months for security and audit purposes.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to: access a copy of your data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention requirements for clinical records; request data portability; and object to or restrict certain processing.

Patients should direct these requests to their dental clinic (the data controller); clinics should contact us at the address below and we will assist within the timelines required by applicable law.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Where data is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent mechanisms recognized under GDPR.

## 10. Sub-processors

We use vetted infrastructure and communication sub-processors (e.g. cloud hosting, SMS/WhatsApp delivery providers) bound by data processing agreements at least as protective as this policy. A current sub-processor list is available on request.

## 11. Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting PHI or personal data, we will notify affected Customers without undue delay and in line with HIPAA (60 days) and GDPR (72 hours to the relevant supervisory authority) requirements, so Customers can meet their own downstream notification obligations.

## 12. Children's Data

Denteex is used by dental clinics to manage pediatric patient records on behalf of parents or guardians who provide such data through the Customer relationship, not directly by children.

## 13. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication and session security (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict where session cookies are in use) and, optionally, privacy-respecting analytics cookies. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We will post material changes here with an updated "Last updated" date and, where required, notify Customers directly.
