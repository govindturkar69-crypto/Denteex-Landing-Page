# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Denteex. This guide walks a new clinic through the core workflow: charting with the 3D odontogram, scheduling, automated reminders, and billing.

## 1. System Requirements

Denteex runs in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on desktop, tablet, or mobile. No installation is required. For the best charting experience, we recommend a device with hardware-accelerated graphics (standard on any laptop or tablet from the last several years).

## 2. Account Setup

1. Create your account from a Book a Demo or Start Free Trial flow.
2. Add your clinic profile: locations, operating hours, and service catalog.
3. Invite staff and assign roles (Dentist, Hygienist, Front Desk, Admin) — permissions scope what each role can view and edit.

## 3. The Interactive 3D Odontogram

The odontogram is Denteex's charting centerpiece, used during diagnosis to record findings tooth-by-tooth.

- **Selecting a tooth** highlights it and opens the condition panel.
- **Cycling conditions** — click a tooth to step through Healthy → Cavity → Filled → Crown states, or select a condition explicitly from the panel for finer detail (e.g. root canal, extraction, implant).
- **Attaching imaging** — drag and drop a digital X-ray onto a tooth to link it directly to that record.
- **History view** — every chart update is timestamped, so you can review how a patient's chart evolved across visits.
- **Exporting** — generate a PDF chart summary for referrals or insurance claims directly from the odontogram toolbar.

## 4. Smart Scheduling & Queue

- Drag-and-drop appointments on the calendar; conflicts and double-bookings are flagged automatically.
- The waiting-room status board shows real-time patient check-in state (Booked → Checked In → In Chair → Complete) visible to front desk and clinical staff simultaneously.
- Online booking can be embedded on your clinic's own website or shared via a QR code for in-office signage.

## 5. Automated Reminders

Configure reminder rules under **Settings → Reminders**:

- Choose channels: Email, SMS, and/or WhatsApp (WhatsApp and SMS are included on Growth and Enterprise; Starter includes unlimited email).
- Set timing: e.g. 48 hours and 2 hours before an appointment, plus a post-treatment check-in.
- Reminders include a one-tap confirm/reschedule link, reducing phone tag and no-shows.

## 6. Billing & Invoicing

- Generate GST-ready invoices, treatment plans, and digital prescriptions directly from a patient's chart in under 30 seconds.
- Submit and track insurance claims from the same billing screen.
- Payment status syncs automatically with the patient record and practice-wide financial reports.

## 7. Patient Portal

Patients can log in to view upcoming appointments, treatment history, and invoices, and to reschedule within your clinic's configured rules — reducing inbound calls to your front desk.

## 8. Reports & Analytics

The analytics dashboard surfaces appointment volume, revenue trends, no-show rates, and (on Growth/Enterprise) multi-location comparisons, updated in real time.

## 9. Tips & Shortcuts

- Press `Esc` to close any modal or dialog without saving.
- Use the global search (top navigation) to jump to any patient record by name or phone number.
- Bulk-select appointments in the calendar to send a one-off reminder batch (e.g. ahead of a holiday closure).

## 10. Troubleshooting

**The odontogram isn't loading smoothly.** Try a hardware-accelerated browser and confirm no browser extension is blocking WebGL.

**Reminders aren't sending.** Confirm your reminder channel is enabled under Settings → Reminders and that the patient's contact details are complete and correctly formatted.

**I need to restore an accidentally deleted record.** Contact support immediately — deleted records are recoverable for a limited window.

## 11. Getting Help

Use the in-app support widget, or see the [FAQ](/faq) for common questions. Enterprise customers have a dedicated account manager and 24/7 priority support.
